# The Sales Falcon — Carousel Brief

Nov 14, 2026 · @Gooseberry

## The philosophy

Every carousel teaches one practical sales idea, and every slide gives the reader something they can say or do on their next call.

- **Who we write for:** sales heads, VPs of sales and sales managers who run teams, B2B and B2C, India first.
- **How we sound:** an experienced sales leader sharing what works. Calm, specific and practical, never a guru.
- **One idea per slide.** If a slide needs two ideas, it becomes two slides.
- **Swap text, not layout.** Each post reuses the templates in The Sales Falcon design system. Only the words change.
- **Independent brand.** We never promote our own products. If PyngDesk appears in a tools post, it is labelled “Our product” on the slide and in the caption's first line.

## Slide by slide

A carousel runs 7 to 10 slides in this order. The objection-handling post (7 slides) is the reference example.

| Slide | Template | Background | What it must do | Example: objection post |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Hook | Navy | Stop the scroll with one tension or problem, 10 words max. Key words stay in the centre square for the profile grid. | “A customer says no. Your next sentence matters.” |
| 2 | Second hook | Ivory or navy | Restate the promise or give the first move. Instagram often re-shows posts from slide 2, so it must stand alone. | “Ask before you answer” |
| 3 to n−2 | Tip, Do/Don't, Conversation, Framework, Metric or Tool card | Ivory (Metric can be navy) | One tip per slide: a headline, the exact words to say in a SAY THIS box, then one line on what to do next. | Price, timing, trust: one objection per slide |
| n−1 | Cheat sheet | Navy | Sum up every tip on one slide. This is the slide people save. | The three SAY THIS lines in short form |
| n (last) | CTA | Navy | One action only: Follow, Save, Share or Comment a keyword. No swipe button. | “Follow @thesalesfalcon” |

## Writing rules

| Element | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Hook headline (slide 1) | 10 words |
| Any other headline | 8 words |
| Body copy | 30 words |
| Script line (words a rep says) | 25 words |
| Label (SAY THIS, PRICE) | 4 words |
| Whole slide | 30 words; split it if it runs over |

- **Say the concrete thing.** “Book the next step live” beats “Optimise your follow-up strategy.”
- **Sentence case everywhere.** All caps only for short labels like SAY THIS or a kicker like OBJECTION HANDLING.
- **Write “you” to the manager**, and “your rep” or “your team” for their people.
- **No hype:** no “crush your quota”, “10x” or “game-changer”, and no emoji on slides.
- **India first:** money in ₹ with lakh notation (₹4.2L). Names and companies are made up but sound real (Meera Iyer, Northwind Foods).
- **Honest numbers:** every stat is sourced in the caption or labelled “Example” on the slide.

## Visual rules

- **Size:** 1080 × 1350 px (4:5 portrait). Margins are 80 px left and right, 96 px top and bottom.
- **Colours:** Midnight Navy #111C26, Warm Ivory #F5F2EA, Champagne Gold #C6A66B. Use at most three colours per slide.
- **Gold** goes on 1 to 3 key words only. On navy it can be text. On ivory it is only ever a fill behind navy text, never gold text.
- **Green and brick** mean do and don't only. They always come with a SAY THIS / DON'T SAY THIS label and a tick or cross.
- **Fonts:** Montserrat for headlines (ExtraBold 112 px on the hook, Bold 72 px elsewhere). Inter for body text (42 px) and script lines (SemiBold Italic, 40 px).
- **On every slide, in the same place:** the slide number (3/7) at top right, @thesalesfalcon at bottom left, and the gold swipe button at bottom right on every slide except the last.
- **Flat look:** no photos, people in suits, shadows, gradients, or screenshots of real apps or chats.

## Caption and final check

The caption is 2 to 3 short lines: the takeaway in plain words, then one CTA that matches the last slide.

Before posting, check every slide:

- [ ] One idea per slide, 30 words or fewer
- [ ] Headlines in sentence case and within their word limits
- [ ] Gold on 3 words or fewer, never as text on ivory
- [ ] Slide number, handle and swipe button in place (no swipe button on the last slide)
- [ ] No hype words, emoji, photos, or real people or companies
- [ ] Only one CTA, on the last slide and in the caption
- [ ] Exported as PNG, 1080 × 1350, named YYYY-MM-DD\_topic-slug\_slideNN.png
